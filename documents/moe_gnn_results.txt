================================================================================
 MoE+GNN  =  MoE with GNN
================================================================================

Name:   MoE+GNN
Script: train_method.sh --method moe_gnn
Folder: outputs/<data>/moe_gnn/<timestamp>/

What it is (one sentence):
  Finished flat MoE → add dual GNN router, keep MoE point cloud, densify OFF,
  train another 30k.

That IS "MoE with GNN". Not a separate model family.

Pipeline:
  1) --method moe
  2) --method moe_gnn   # loads moe MLPs + ply automatically

Kitchen / stump gain-split (best arm):
  kitchen  moe 31.865 → moe_gnn 32.101  (+0.24)
  stump    moe 26.663 → moe_gnn 26.802  (+0.14)
